Specialist Male Only Care

Moorland Suite

An Elegant Haven for Gentlemen

Our Moorland Suite, is dedicated to providing a male only environment. There are 28 bedrooms all of which have accessible ensuite bathrooms. There are large communal spaces which all have exquisite views of the majestic Peak District.

The Moorland suite is unique and offers targeted support, care and activities suited for male residents. The team members are by and large male staff members.

We provide a hydration station that encourages residents to drink whenever they wish and an opportunity to enjoy a variety of snacks. To ensure that our residents are supported in these communal spaces, there is a nursing station where trained staff are ready to assist should the need arise.

The suite has its own entrance with good access to parking for family, friends and those involved in the resident’s care. Bedrooms and day spaces boasts of scenic views of the stunning peak district and there are several larger rooms. For those of our residents who enjoy spending time outside, we have a spacious private gardens which offer the opportunity to embrace the outdoors.

Community Spaces

Our community spaces are designed to provide a place where our residents can interact with each other and socialise. The Moorland Suite has a large, spacious, and bright lounge which is perfect for sitting and relaxing or for getting involved in the activities held there. All spaces are decorated in dementia aware tones, creating a warm, inviting atmosphere for everyone.

There is a hydration station that is designed to encourage residents to drink whenever they choose to. A well-stocked kitchenette also forms part of the space to ensure that food is available throughout the day with access to the main kitchen as required. Our nurses station has well trained staff who are able to assist as needed.

Private, Tailored Bedrooms

Each ensuite bedroom is carefully furnished to provide a safe and sound personal space for each of our residents. We encourage residents together with their family, friends and others who are involved in their care to personalise their bedroom.

The rooms are spacious, providing space for personal possessions and mementos. With safety being of paramount importance, each bedroom is fitted with a range of technological support aids which include a nurse call system, sensor mats and a host of other aids to meet the individual safety needs of residents.

Secure, Healing Gardens

The Moorland Suite has large gardens that are both private and secure. The gardens have an impressive array of mature trees, shrubs and flowers. Residents in this area, have access to these outdoor spaces.

Here they can become involved in gardening and other outdoor activities. There are seating areas that offer a place to sit and enjoy the fresh air, relax, and take in the natural, calming beauty of the Peak District which surrounds this suite.
