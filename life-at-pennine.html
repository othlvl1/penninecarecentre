Life at Pennine

Nourishing body, mind, and soul through dedicated care and vibrant activities.

Person Centred Care

We believe that every resident is an individual. From the moment we meet, we build a trusting, supportive relationship to ensure unique preferences remain at the heart of everything we do.

Best Services

Understanding unique and individual preferences ensures that residents remain in the center of all that we do. We strive to provide a safe, sound, and supportive relationship from day one.

Giving Importance

From choice of décor to meal options, we engage with residents and loved ones to ensure voices are heard and acted upon. We actively welcome feedback to continuously improve our bespoke care.

Family & Community

We actively encourage families to be involved in the life of their loved ones. Transparency and open communication are the cornerstones of our community.

Newsletter

A regular, informative newsletter helps families gain deep insight into the vibrant happenings and updates at Pennine.

Private Page

Our dedicated private Facebook group allows families to see regular updates and images of their loved ones' daily journey.

Family Meetings

Regular meetings led by our Wellbeing Manager ensure that family opinions shape the ongoing development of care plans.

Innovative Care

We combine cutting-edge assistive technology with essential human compassion.

Assistive Tech

Reviewing and building on our use of technology enables us to deploy systems like nurse calls and sensor mats in a less intrusive but highly effective way.

Safety & Support

Technology in no way replaces the human aspect of our work, but it increases the level of detection and support for the safety of our residents.
