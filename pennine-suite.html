A Welcoming, Supportive Environment

Pennine Suite

An Introduction to Tasteful Modernity

Nestling in the beautiful market town of Glossop, the Pennine Suite has all the charm that one might expect of a place, whilst retaining some of its original hand-crafted features, blends this with tasteful modernity.

The Pennine Suite is a mixed gender space that has 30, beautifully and sensitively decorated bedrooms, where residents and their families have a canvas on which to create a room that is individual to them; with ample space for pictures and treasured mementos.

Bedrooms and day spaces boasts of scenic views of the stunning peak district and there are several larger rooms, with some ensuite bathrooms. There are a number of comfortable and homely meeting rooms where families, friends and those involved in the care of our residents can sit, talk and spend precious quality time together.

For those of our residents who enjoy spending time outside, we have a spacious mature gardens which offer the opportunity to embrace the extensive range of flowers, shrubs and trees, or to sit and admire these gardens with impressive, rolling hills as a backdrop.

Our Warm & Caring Community

We recognise the importance of community and as such, we have a large lounge area where our residents can spend time together, socialise, engage with the range of activities that happen there, or simply gaze out of the picture windows onto green fields where horses, livestock and birds can be seen.

The spacious dining rooms are set out to create a warm and friendly dining atmosphere. Tables are decorated with tablecloths and clustered to allow for communal eating experiences, which can also be personalised to meet the individual needs of residents.

We also provide a hydration station that encourages residents to drink whenever they wish and an opportunity to enjoy a variety of snacks. To ensure that our residents are supported in these communal spaces, there is a nursing station where trained staff are ready to assist should the need arise.

In addition to this, there are also quiet lounges where our residents can enjoy a smaller space which is both peaceful and private.

Lifts are built within the Pennine Suite, making all areas and lounges comfortably accessible.

Private, Well-Appointed Bedrooms

Our well-appointed bedrooms are spacious and offer an opportunity to personalise them. We encourage our residents and their loved ones to furnish and decorate their room to their taste.

Some of the bedrooms have ensuite bathrooms and all have views of the Peak District.

As the safety of our residents is of paramount importance, each room has a range of technological support which include, nurse call systems, sensor mats and other aids as individually required to meet the individual safety needs of residents.

Tranquil Gardens & Outdoors

With well placed seating, our residents can, should they wish, spend time in the very pleasant surroundings made up of mature trees, shrubs, and flowers.

Throughout the Summer, there are organised activities in the gardens which lend themselves well to the benefits of fresh air, socialising with others, combined with the breathtaking views of the Peak District.

Coming Soon Wellbeing Space

As part of our modernisation plans for 2024, we are currently building a wellbeing space. Our wellbeing space will offer a range of in-house activities that include chiropody, hairdressing, and nail bar for manicures.

Here, residents can receive pamper sessions which we envisage will greatly increase their sense of wellbeing, peace of mind, and relaxation.
